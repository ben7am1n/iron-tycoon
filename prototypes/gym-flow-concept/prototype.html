The idea

Members walk in from the left, pick a machine (avoiding crowded ones), use it, and leave. The 3 machines start clumped in the corner — watch them jam up. Spread the machines out and watch the flow smooth and the pink congestion fade.

Toggle the heatmap to see crowding. Warmer = busier. The ⛔ marker means a machine is walled off / unreachable.
